**Building engineering** focuses on buildings as complete systems. Concordia describes it as bridging architecture and engineering: structure, but also the building envelope, heating and cooling, energy, lighting, acoustics and electrical systems, with a strong focus on energy-efficient and net-zero buildings.

**Civil engineering** covers infrastructure more broadly: bridges, tunnels, roads and transportation, water resources and environmental systems, as well as building structures.

In short, a civil engineer might design the bridge, while a building engineer makes sure a building is safe, comfortable and efficient from foundation to roof.
