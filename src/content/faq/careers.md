Building engineers work in consulting firms, construction companies, architecture firms, government and real estate. Typical roles include:

- Building envelope engineer
- Energy efficiency analyst or energy modeller
- Building systems (mechanical/electrical) engineer
- Structural engineer
- Construction manager or cost engineer
- Building inspector
- Sustainability consultant

Concordia lists employers of its graduates such as WSP, Stantec, CIMA+, AECOM, Pomerleau, Kiewit, Hydro-Québec, the STM and EXO. The program is accredited by Engineers Canada, so graduates can work toward their P.Eng.
