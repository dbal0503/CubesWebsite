Building engineering students choose one of two options:

- **Building Energy and Environment:** energy-efficient design, HVAC, building science and sustainability
- **Building Structures and Construction:** structural design and construction engineering

Concordia also offers an integrated path that counts courses toward a Master of Engineering.
