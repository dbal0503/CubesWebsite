CUBES (Concordia University Building Engineering Society) represents Concordia's undergraduate building engineering students. We organize experiences that go beyond the classroom:

- **ASP courses** (health and safety on the construction site), with part of the fee covered
- **Networking cocktails** with special guests and recruiters
- **Visits** to building-technology conferences and expos
- **Software tutorials** for AutoCAD and Revit
- **Design and consulting competitions** for hands-on problem-solving
- **Game nights** and social activities

All our funds go to organizing the events and workshops students need.
