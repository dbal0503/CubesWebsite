The standard program runs four years (eight terms). A rough outline:

- **Year 1:** programming, drawing and design, statics, dynamics, mechanics of materials, thermodynamics
- **Year 2:** structural analysis, building engineering systems, building science, fluid mechanics, steel and wood design
- **Year 3:** reinforced concrete, thermal analysis, soil mechanics, building service systems, construction engineering, a design project
- **Year 4:** building envelope, HVAC design, a two-term capstone project, and technical electives

There are September, January and co-op entry sequences. The official course sequence PDF has the exact courses for each term.
